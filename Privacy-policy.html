Privacy Policy
Last Updated: May 2026

1. INTRODUCTION

Namma Cable Systems ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Namma Cable mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard details about operators, staff, and customer accounts in our system.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

  • Customer Profiles: Name, contact number, address, setup box details, and connection status.
  • Payment Logs: Transaction history, collected amounts, unpaid balances, and date/time of collection.
  • Operator Profile Data: Login credentials, area performance logs, and daily closing details.
  • Device Info: Offline synchronization logs and local cash registry metadata.

3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use the collected information for essential operational purposes:

  • To log daily billing and payments.
  • To synchronize offline collections to the database.
  • To generate bills, manage customer packages, and assign support tasks.
  • To monitor and analyze area performance.

4. DATA SECURITY

Your data safety is our highest priority:

  • Access control: Features are restricted based on authorized credentials.
  • Data Sync Security: Subscriptions are uploaded via secure protocols (HTTPS) to the database.
  • Local Cache: Local database files are stored securely on your device.

5. DEVICE PERMISSIONS

The application requires permissions to function correctly:

  • Network: To sync data, check connections, and fetch dynamic packages.
  • Storage: To cache offline databases and save reports.
  • Notifications: To inform about billing updates and sync success.
